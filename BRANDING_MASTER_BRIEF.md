## 1. Executive Summary

**Anviora** is the Digital Product Identity Platform — an AI-powered infrastructure layer that helps Indian battery and textile manufacturers create, verify, and track digital product passports for global export.

We transform compliance from a cost center into a competitive advantage. Every battery and textile product gets a unique digital identity — a passport that proves provenance, verifies compliance, and unlocks access to European markets.

Our platform is the **only** India-born solution that handles **both** domestic EPR compliance (BIS, CPCB, BWMR) **and** EU export requirements (Battery Regulation 2023/1542, ESPR) in a single, affordable SaaS product.

The EU Battery Passport becomes mandatory on **18 February 2027** — less than 9 months away. We are building the default platform Indian manufacturers will use to stay in business.

> **What we need from you:** A complete brand identity system — logo, color palette, typography, iconography, illustration style, and UI component aesthetics — that works across web apps, desktop apps, mobile apps, and a marketing website. The brand must feel credible to European buyers yet accessible to Indian factory owners.

## 4. Brand Positioning

### 4.1 Repositioning: From "Compliance" to "Digital Product Identity"

Shift the narrative from "consultancy" to **digital product infrastructure**. Emphasize *battery passports* as an embedded identity layer, not just paperwork. EU regulations mandate per-battery digital passports with full traceability (identification, performance data, carbon footprint, etc.).

Highlight that Anviora is **"battery intelligence infrastructure"** rather than a form-filling service. This reframes the product as a **Stripe/Vercel-style platform** rather than a consulting service.

**Before:** "We help you fill compliance forms."  
**After:** "We provide the identity infrastructure that every battery needs to enter global markets."

### 4.2 The Golden Circle

**WHY (Purpose)**
> Indian manufacturers deserve a fair shot at global markets. Compliance shouldn't be a walled garden only big corporations can afford.

**HOW (Differentiation)**
- AI reads certificates and auto-fills compliance forms
- One platform for EU Battery Regulation + India BWM Rules + Textile standards
- Blockchain-anchored certificates buyers can verify in seconds
- QR-coded Battery Passports that work anywhere in Europe
- Alerts before certificates expire
- Native Tally integration (no other global competitor has this)
- DGFT TRACE subsidy claim automation

**WHAT (Product)**
> Anviora is the Digital Product Identity Platform that helps Indian battery and textile manufacturers generate EU-compliant product passports, track certificates, and manage product identity across the entire lifecycle.

### 4.3 Positioning Statement
> "For Indian battery and textile exporters who struggle with complex EU and domestic compliance, Anviora is the Digital Product Identity Platform that automates documentation, tracks certificates, and generates QR-coded product passports — unlike expensive consultants or fragmented spreadsheets, we give you real-time compliance visibility at a fraction of the cost."

### 4.4 Key Brand Attributes

| Attribute | What It Means | What It Does NOT Mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Authoritative** | We understand regulations deeply | Boring, bureaucratic, government-style |
| **Approachable** | Anyone can use it, no compliance degree needed | Casual, unprofessional, sloppy |
| **Trustworthy** | Your data is safe, your certificates are valid | Stodgy, old-fashioned, slow |
| **Indian** | Built here, for our people, by our people | Cheap, low-quality, reductive |
| **Global-ready** | Passes EU standards, impresses European buyers | Pretentious, trying too hard to be European |
| **Technically advanced** | AI, blockchain, real-time alerts | Crypto-bro, hype-driven, vaporware |

### 4.5 Brand Personality
Think: **Linear.app meets RBI meets Bloomberg Green.**

- Clean and confident, not flashy
- Expert but not condescending
- Modern but not frivolous
- Green-tech but not hippie
- Indian but not parochial

## 6. Design System v1 (Locked Direction)

**Status:** This section represents the locked design direction. The branding and product teams should build against these specifications.

### 6.1 Brand Personality (Locked)

Anviora is not a compliance company.

Anviora is infrastructure.

We help manufacturers create digital identities for physical products.

**The feeling should be:**
- Intelligent
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Technical
- Modern
- Global

**Not:**
- Government
- Enterprise legacy software
- Consulting agency
- Logistics company

### 6.2 Visual Direction (Locked)

**Keywords:** Infrastructure, Identity, Traceability, Verification, Trust

**Visual references:** Linear, Clay, Vercel, Stripe

**Avoid:**
- Battery illustrations
- Factory stock photos
- Handshake imagery
- Compliance checklists

### 6.3 Layout Principles (Locked)

#### Large Typography
Headlines should feel oversized.

**Examples:**
- Every Product Needs an Identity.
- Build Trust Into Every Product.
- Digital Product Passports for Modern Manufacturing.

**Font sizes:**
- Hero: 72px–96px (consider 120px display size for absolute top impact)
- Section Titles: 48px–64px
- Body: 18px–20px

#### White Space
Every section breathes. No crowded layouts. No dashboard screenshots squeezed into cards.

Think: **50% content, 50% whitespace**

#### Product First
Homepage should show product within first viewport. Do not hide screenshots. Do not use random illustrations.

Users should instantly see:
- Passport Dashboard
- Verification Page
- QR Tracking
- Analytics

### 6.4 Color System (Locked)

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | `#FFFFFF` | Primary surface |
| **Primary Text** | `#0F172A` | Headlines, body text |
| **Secondary Text** | `#64748B` | Captions, metadata, placeholders |
| **Border** | `#E2E8F0` | Dividers, card borders, input borders |
| **Primary Accent** | `#4F46E5` | Links, buttons, active states, indigo highlights |
| **Success** | `#10B981` | Positive status, checkmarks, compliant badges |
| **Warning** | `#F59E0B` | Certificate expiry soon, compliance dropping |
| **Danger** | `#EF4444` | Errors, expired, critical alerts, blocked status |
| **Info** | `#0EA5E9` | Neutral alerts, tips, informational states |
| **Dark Hero** | `#09090B` | Marketing homepage hero background |

**Avoid bright greens commonly used by compliance products.**

**Dark mode adjustments (product UI):**
- Background → `#0F0F10`
- Card surface → `#18181B`
- Primary text → `#FAFAFA`
- Secondary text → `#A1A1AA`
- Border → `#27272A`

### 6.5 Typography (Locked)

| Role | Font | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Geist | All UI text, headlines, body |
| **Secondary** | Inter | Fallback, long-form reading, marketing body copy |
| **Monospace** | Geist Mono | Data, code, certificate numbers, batch IDs, tables |

**Rules:**
- Bold headlines
- Minimal font weights (400, 500, 600, 700 only)
- No decorative fonts
- Tight tracking on headlines (-0.02em)

**Type scale:**
- Display: 120px / 700 / -0.03em
- H1: 96px / 700 / -0.02em
- H2: 72px / 700 / -0.02em
- H3: 48px / 600 / -0.01em
- H4: 32px / 600 / -0.01em
- H5: 24px / 600 / 0
- Body Large: 20px / 400 / 0 / line-height 1.6
- Body: 18px / 400 / 0 / line-height 1.6
- Body Small: 16px / 400 / 0 / line-height 1.5
- Caption: 14px / 400 / 0 / line-height 1.4
- Mono: 14px / 400 / 0 / line-height 1.4

### 6.6 Navigation (Locked)

**Nav items:** Products | Industries | Pricing | Resources | Book Demo

**Navigation height:** 72px

**Behavior:**
- Floating blur background when scrolling (`bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-md`)
- Subtle bottom border (`border-b border-gray-100`)
- Logo left, links center, CTA right
- Mobile: hamburger menu with full-screen overlay

### 6.7 Cards (Locked)

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Border Radius | 16px |
| Border | 1px solid `#E2E8F0` |
| Shadow | Very subtle — `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)` |
| Padding | 24px |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` |

**Rules:**
- Never stack dozens of cards
- Use whitespace between cards, not heavy shadows
- Hover: subtle lift (translateY -2px) + shadow intensify

### 6.8 Icons (Locked)

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Library | Lucide React |
| Stroke | 1.5px |
| Color | Monochrome (current text color) |
| Size | 16px, 20px, 24px standard |

**Rules:**
- No colorful icons
- No filled icons (unless active state)
- No custom icon sets

### 6.9 Motion (Locked)

**Allowed:**
- Fade in (opacity 0→1)
- Slide up (translateY 20px→0)
- Counter animations (numbers counting up)
- Progress indicators
- Button hover transitions (200ms ease)
- Card lift on hover

**Never use:**
- Parallax
- Spinning objects
- 3D battery models
- Heavy Lottie animations
- Page transitions longer than 300ms

**Timing:**
- Micro-interactions: 150–200ms
- Entrance animations: 300–400ms
- Stagger delay: 50–100ms per item
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (ease-out)

### 6.10 Dashboard Design (Locked)

**Reference:** Linear + Attio

**Rules:**
- No heavy shadows
- No gradients
- Thin borders (1px `#E2E8F0`)
- Large tables with comfortable row height (48px)
- Generous spacing between sections
- Command palette (Cmd+K)
- Keyboard shortcuts
- Dark sidebar (`#09090B` or `#0F0F10`)
- Light content area (`#FFFFFF`)

### 6.11 Product Naming Architecture (Locked)

| Level | Name |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Anviora |
| **Product 1** | Anviora Battery |
| **Product 2** | Anviora Textile |

When someone lands on Anviora, they should think:

## 12. Logo Concepts

### 12.1 "Dual Circle Passport" (Iconic)
Two overlapping circles or rings (symbolizing the two ends of the supply chain, or battery + globe) forming a stylized *"A"* or *battery shape.* One circle could hint at a battery cell and the other at a fingerprint/QR pattern. This represents *two entities connecting through a digital passport*.

**Assessment:** Strongest concept. Scales well, abstract but meaningful. Easy to animate. Works as favicon and app icon.

### 12.2 "Digital Chain" (Geometric)
A minimal, interlocking chain link or hexagon pattern forming a battery-like outline. Emphasizes *blockchain anchoring and security.* A stylized chain link that subtly shapes into the letter *A* (for Anviora), or a battery cell outline made from connected nodes.

**Assessment:** Over-indexes on blockchain. Blockchain is a feature, not the brand. Use with caution.

### 12.3 "Battery & Checkmark" (Seal of Trust)
A battery icon whose negative end forms a checkmark or shield. The positive terminal plus shape becomes a check. Suggests *certification and trust.* The brand name uses a friendly sans-serif font, with a green check integrated.

**Assessment:** Too literal, feels like a 2010s app icon. Won't age well. Risk of looking like a battery tester app.

### 12.4 "Passcode Plate" (Techy Mono)
A square or rounded-square icon containing a QR-code–like matrix or circuit lines that subtly form "AI" or "AV" (for Anviora). Highlights *digital identity and data.* Rendered in one color (negative space) for a modern look.

**Assessment:** Too busy for a favicon. Reads as "security app," not "manufacturing platform."

**Recommendation:** Explore the **Dual Circle** further. Also consider a mark based on **traceability/connection** rather than battery shape — you're expanding to textiles, so a battery-centric logo becomes a liability in 12 months.

## 15. Implementation Notes

### 15.1 Design System & UI Components

Build a component library (buttons, forms, cards) following an updated style guide.

- **Color palette:** Primary (`#0F172A`), accent (`#4F46E5`), success (`#10B981`), warning (`#F59E0B`), danger (`#EF4444`)
- **Typography:** Geist for UI, Geist Mono for data, Inter for long-form
- **Spacing:** Generous line-height and whitespace; 50% content, 50% whitespace
- **Borders:** 1px solid `#E2E8F0`; no heavy borders; no gradients in UI
- **Cards:** 16px radius, 24px padding, very subtle shadow
- **Icons:** Lucide, 1.5px stroke, monochrome

### 15.2 Imagery & Illustration

- Favor *abstract, data-inspired visuals* over stock photos
- Use vector illustrations of network nodes, connection lines, identity patterns
- Organic gradients (like Stripe's wave) can add energy on marketing pages only
- Icons should be simple line drawings (no skeuomorphic art)
- Include screenshots of actual product UI where helpful, annotated if needed
- If photography is used, focus on clean factory, lab, or mobility scenes with cool color grade
- **Avoid:** battery illustrations, factory stock photos, handshake imagery, compliance checklists

### 15.3 Motion & Animation

- Apply subtle micro-animations to delight
- Examples: hover effects on buttons (color transition, 200ms), gentle progress bar, animated illustrations in hero
- Use motion purposefully (e.g. a checkmark swipe-in after "upload complete")
- Keep animations short (<0.5s) and optional for reduced-motion modes
- Onboarding or empty states could have lighthearted illustrations or explanatory tooltips
- **Never:** parallax, spinning objects, 3D battery models, heavy Lottie animations

### 15.4 Accessibility

- Ensure high contrast text (WCAG AA compliance)
- All images need meaningful alt text
- Use semantic HTML (headings, lists) for SEO and accessibility
- Interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable with visible focus outlines
- Forms need labels and error messaging
- Test with screen readers
- Support `prefers-reduced-motion`

### 15.5 SEO Considerations

- Write meaningful headings (H1, H2, H3) reflecting user search intent (e.g. *"EU Battery Compliance Software"*, *"Digital Product Passports"*)
- Include keyword-rich page titles/meta descriptions (e.g. "Anviora – AI Platform for Battery Passports & Compliance")
- Use descriptive link text (not just "click here")
- Provide schema markup for organization/contact
- Publish blog/resource content to target long-tail queries (e.g. "how to generate battery passport india")

### 15.6 Technical Constraints

- **Web:** Next.js 15 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4 + shadcn/ui
- **Desktop:** Tauri v2 (Rust + WebView)
- **Mobile:** React Native + Expo
- **Performance:** Lighthouse 95+, LCP < 2.5s, CLS = 0, INP < 100ms
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA
