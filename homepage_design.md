🧠 THE CORE PROBLEM (UPDATED DIAGNOSIS)

Your homepage does not suffer from weak copy or missing sections.

Your problem is layout signal.

Right now:

Too many sections share the same visual weight

The eye has no clear anchors or pauses

Strong statements sit next to equally strong statements

Everything feels important → nothing feels decisive

This causes cognitive flattening:

The reader understands the words, but doesn’t feel the hierarchy.

Industry-leading infra pages succeed because they:

Control contrast rhythm

Separate statements from explanations

Use space as authority

Treat layout like an engineering document, not marketing

Your content is correct.
Your layout needs discipline and cadence.

🧱 THE SOLUTION: THE “INFRA RHYTHM” LAYOUT SYSTEM (UPDATED)

Think of the page as an engineering report, not a story.

Each section must serve one function only.

The correct rhythm:

ASSERTION
↓
JUSTIFICATION
↓
EVIDENCE
↓
BREATHING SPACE
↓
ASSERTION
↓
DETAIL
↓
EVIDENCE
↓
RESOLUTION


Rules of this system:

Assertions are short, bold, and isolated

Justifications are narrower and quieter

Evidence is visual or structured

Breathing space is intentional emptiness

Never stack two assertions back-to-back

This rhythm is what makes Stripe, Linear, and engineering documentation feel inevitable.
