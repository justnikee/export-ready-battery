## What we did
Chose **one rule**: QR ZIP and label PDF are production artifacts. Issue only after activation (`ACTIVE` or `ARCHIVED`). Never on `DRAFT`.

Why not “QR on draft, labels after activate”: a QR on a pack is as official as a printed label. Activation is the quota + compliance gate. In-app View still works on draft. ARCHIVED is allowed because quota was already paid (UI already treated archived as not-draft).

Enforced on:
- JWT `GET /batches/{id}/download` (QR) — was unrestricted
- JWT `GET /batches/{id}/labels` — was ACTIVE-only (archived 403)
- API-key `GET /external/batches/{id}/labels` — had no status check
- Export jobs `QR_ZIP` / `LABELS_PDF` create + process

UI: Downloads tab already locked both on draft; copy now says why. Batch ⋯ menu no longer disables QR/labels on archived.
