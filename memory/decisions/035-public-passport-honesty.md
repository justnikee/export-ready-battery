# ADR 035 — Public passport display honesty

Date: 2026-10-31. Status: accepted for draft review.

The public battery record must identify itself as manufacturer-issued, not an official registry, regulatory filing, or external certification. Treat the BPAN as our serial identifier. Uploaded documents and India EPR/BIS numbers are manufacturer-declared unless independently verified. On the public page, explain that repair and dismantling detail is restricted; partner/regulator access remains separate. Display absent carbon-class, recycled-content, and due-diligence fields without implying a current February 2027 failure.

The [European Commission battery passport FAQ](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/eu-digital-product-passport-faq-batteries_en) states that carbon footprint, recycled-content, and due-diligence information will not yet be required when battery passports begin in February 2027. Keep date-dependent copy under review before production publication.
