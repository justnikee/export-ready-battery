Title: India Battery Waste Management Rules 2022
Source: India_BWM_Rules_2022.pdf
Section: Rule 4 - Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)
---
Every Producer of batteries shall have the obligation of Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) for the batteries that they place in the market. 
The EPR targets require the Producer to ensure recycling and recovery of battery waste.
Producers must register with the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) on the online portal and obtain an EPR Registration Number.
No Producer shall manufacture or import batteries without obtaining a valid CPCB EPR Registration Number.
Producers are required to file annual returns regarding the quantity of batteries manufactured, imported, and the waste collected and sent for recycling.

Title: India Battery Waste Management Rules 2022
Source: India_BWM_Rules_2022.pdf
Section: Rule 10 - Mandatory Minimum Recycled Material Use Targets
---
Producers must ensure a minimum percentage of recycled materials is used in the manufacturing of new batteries.
The targets are progressive and depend on the battery type:
For lead-acid batteries, the minimum recycled material percentage must be:
- 2024-2025: 30% recycled lead
- 2026-2027: 40% recycled lead
- 2028-2029: 50% recycled lead
For lithium-ion batteries:
- 2026-2027: 5% recycled materials (lithium, cobalt, nickel)
- 2028-2029: 10% recycled materials
Waste recyclers must certify the recovery process and provide verification certificates to Producers.
