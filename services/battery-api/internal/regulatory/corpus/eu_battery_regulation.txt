Title: EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542
Source: EU_2023_1542.pdf
Section: Article 7 - Carbon Footprint of Electric Vehicle Batteries
---
For electric vehicle batteries, rechargeable industrial batteries with a capacity greater than 2 kWh, and LMT batteries, a carbon footprint declaration shall be drawn up for each battery model per manufacturing plant. 
The carbon footprint declaration shall include at least the following information:
(a) administrative information concerning the manufacturer;
(b) information about the battery model;
(c) information about the geographic location of the battery manufacturing facility;
(d) the carbon footprint of the battery, calculated as kg of CO2 equivalent per one kWh of the total energy provided by the battery over its expected service life;
(e) the carbon footprint performance class to which the battery model belongs (classes A to G);
(f) a web link to the public version of the study supporting the carbon footprint values.
The carbon footprint calculation must follow the rules set out in Annex II, using life-cycle assessment (LCA) methodology.

Title: EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542
Source: EU_2023_1542.pdf
Section: Article 8 - Recycled Content in Industrial and EV Batteries
---
From August 2028, industrial batteries, EV batteries, and LMT batteries with a capacity greater than 2 kWh that contain cobalt, lead, lithium, or nickel in active materials shall be accompanied by documentation containing information on the share of cobalt, lead, lithium, or nickel recovered from waste present in active materials in each battery model.
By August 2031, the share of recovered materials shall reach the following minimum targets:
- Cobalt: 16% recycled content recovery
- Lithium: 6% recycled content recovery
- Nickel: 6% recycled content recovery
- Lead: 85% recycled content recovery
Documentation must prove the recycling source and verify the calculations using the methodology defined by the European Commission.

Title: EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542
Source: EU_2023_1542.pdf
Section: Article 11 - Removability and Replaceability of Portable Batteries
---
Any natural or legal person that places on the market products incorporating portable batteries shall ensure that those batteries are readily removable and replaceable by the end-user at any time during the lifetime of the product.
A portable battery is considered readily removable by the end-user where it can be removed from a product with the use of commercially available tools, without requiring the use of specialized tools, unless provided free of charge, or proprietary tools, thermal energy, or solvents to disassemble.
This requirement does not apply where continuity of power supply is necessary and a permanent connection between the product and the portable battery is required for safety, performance, medical, or data integrity reasons.
For LMT batteries, they must be removable and replaceable by independent professionals.
