## 📡 Seed Signal 1: Regulatory Landscape (Primary Driver)

### 1.1 EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542) — Active Enforcement

- **Feb 2025**: Carbon footprint declarations became mandatory for EV batteries placed on the EU market.
- **Aug 2025**: Supply chain due diligence polices required for battery manufacturers.
- **Jan 2026**: Market surveillance authorities began actively checking battery DPP compliance. Non-compliant batteries face product withdrawal from EU market.
- **Feb 2027**: Full Digital Battery Passport becomes legally mandatory for all EV batteries, LMT batteries, and industrial batteries >2 kWh sold in the EU. Each battery must carry a QR code linking to a verified digital passport containing carbon footprint, material composition, recycled content, state of health, and performance data.
- **Impact**: Any battery manufacturer (regardless of country of origin) selling into the EU must comply or lose market access.

### 1.2 EU Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR) — Product Passport Expansion

- **Jul 2026**: EU central digital registry for Digital Product Passports (DPPs) is scheduled to become operational.
- **2027**: Textiles sold in the EU will require a "minimal and simplified DPP" — covering fiber composition, hazardous substances, and essential labeling. Delegated act for textiles was finalized in January 2025.
- **2028–2030**: Advanced DPP requirements for textiles including environmental impact assessments and detailed supply chain mapping.
- **2033**: Full circular DPPs required for textiles.
- **Upcoming categories**: Electronics, furniture, construction materials — all expected to receive DPP mandates under ESPR in phased rollouts from 2027 onward.
- **Key principle**: DPPs apply to ALL brands selling in the EU market, regardless of country of origin.

### 1.3 India — Battery Waste Management Rules & EPR

- **Feb 2025**: Battery Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2025 notified by MoEFCC. Scope expanded to ALL battery categories (portable, automotive, EV, industrial). Obligations now extend to e-commerce platforms.
- **EPR Targets**: Material recovery targets of 70% (2024–25), 80% (2026), and 90% (2026–27 onward).
- **QR Code Mandate**: Producers must print QR codes displaying EPR registration number on batteries, packs, equipment, and packaging.
- **CPCB Online Portal**: Centralized portal for EPR compliance, recycling certificate tracking, and producer registration. Quarterly compliance monitoring.
- **Sep 2025**: Government approved incentive scheme to support recycling of e-waste and Li-ion battery scrap — 270 kilotonnes recycling capacity target FY2025–2031.
- **Delhi EV Policy 2.0 (Mar 2026)**: EV battery recycling framework under development, DPCC as nodal agency.
- **India Gap**: A full "battery passport" system (EU-style) is recognized as a critical need but not yet fully regulated. This represents a FIRST-MOVER opportunity for AnvayaLabs in the Indian market.

### 1.4 Other Emerging Regulations

- **US**: No federal DPP mandate yet, but SEC climate disclosure rules, California SB 253 (Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act), and increasing ESG investor pressure are creating voluntary compliance demand.
- **China**: GB/T standards for battery traceability being drafted. China's New Energy Vehicle (NEV) industry is the world's largest — any DPP-type regulation would create massive demand.
- **Japan & South Korea**: Both exploring battery lifecycle management regulations aligned with EU frameworks.
- **ASEAN**: Thailand and Indonesia (major battery manufacturing hubs) are watching EU developments closely.

## 🧬 Seed Signal 4: AnvayaLabs — Current Product DNA

### 4.1 Platform Capabilities (As of March 2026)

- **Digital Battery Passports**: UUID-based identifiers, QR code generation (256×256 PNG), public verification pages.
- **Batch Management**: Create batches with chemistry/voltage/capacity specs, bulk CSV upload, auto-serial numbers with market-specific prefixes, ZIP download of QR codes.
- **Lifecycle Tracking**: Action-based transitions (manufacture → quality-check → ship → install → maintain → recycle), role-guarded, evidence-validated, full audit logging.
- **Multi-Market Compliance**: EU (2023/1542) and India (PLI, BIS, EPR) with market-specific forms.
- **EPR Calculator**: Automatic EPR obligation calculation from batch/production data.
- **Carbon Footprint Calculator**: JRC-aligned emission factors from BOM inputs (cell chemistry, capacity, source country, transport mode, weight).
- **B2B Integrations**: Webhooks, telemetry ingestion, bulk export jobs for ERP integration.
- **Team Management**: RBAC (Owner, Admin, Member), email invitations, seat limits by plan.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time production stats, QR scan tracking with geolocation, export activity feeds.

### 4.3 Expansion Vision — Universal Product Passport Engine

AnvayaLabs is evolving from a battery-focused compliance tool into a **universal Product Passport Engine** that supports:

1. **Batteries** (current) → EU 2023/1542 + India BWM Rules
2. **Textiles** (next) → EU ESPR textile DPP (2027 mandate)

## 👥 Seed Signal 5: Stakeholder Profiles (For Agent Persona Generation)

### 5.1 Manufacturers (Primary Users)

- **Indian battery manufacturers** (100+ registered with BIS): Need compliance with both Indian regulations AND EU DPP to export to Europe. Currently using spreadsheets and manual processes. Pain point: navigating dual-compliance.
- **Indian textile exporters**: $165B industry, world's 2nd largest. EU DPP mandate in 2027 will force adoption. Most have zero DPP readiness. Pain point: understanding what data to collect and how to digitize supply chain.
- **EU/Global battery OEMs**: Under active enforcement pressure. Looking for cost-effective solutions. Pain point: integrating DPP with existing ERP/MES systems.
- **Chinese battery manufacturers**: World's largest battery producers. Need EU DPP compliance to maintain market access. Open to SaaS solutions that simplify compliance.

### 5.2 Regulators

- **EU Commission (DG GROW)**: Pushing enforcement of battery DPP, building central digital registry (operational Jul 2026). Priority: interoperability and data integrity.
- **CPCB (India)**: Managing centralized EPR portal, monitoring compliance quarterly. Priority: digital traceability and producer accountability.
- **BIS (India)**: Enforcing IS 17855:2022 for Li-ion batteries. Priority: certification verification.

### 5.3 Supply Chain Partners

- **Recyclers**: Need access to battery composition data, SoH (State of Health), and lifecycle history to optimize recycling processes. EU mandates 65% recycling efficiency for Li-based batteries.
- **PROs (Producer Responsibility Organizations)**: Manage collective EPR obligations. Need digital tracking of certificate purchases against obligations.
- **Raw material suppliers**: Increasingly required to provide due diligence data on sourcing (conflict minerals, child labor, environmental impact).

### 5.4 Investors & Ecosystem

- **Climate tech VCs**: Actively looking for GreenTech SaaS plays. DPP market's 33–39% CAGR is highly attractive.
- **Government accelerators**: India's Startup India, DPIIT, and MeitY programs support compliance-tech startups.
- **Industry associations**: IESA (India Energy Storage Alliance), BatteryInfo Europe, EUROBAT.

### 5.5 End Consumers

- **EV buyers**: Increasingly want to verify battery origin, carbon footprint, and recyclability before purchase.
- **Conscious consumers (textiles)**: Demand transparency on fiber composition, worker conditions, and environmental impact.
- **B2B procurement teams**: Require DPP data as part of supplier qualification and ESG reporting.
